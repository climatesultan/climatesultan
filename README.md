🌏 My Journey

I am an Ayurveda student and self-directed learner exploring the connections between Ayurveda, biodiversity, traditional and Indigenous knowledge, climate action, culture, technology and communities.

I learn by studying deeply — and then I try to turn what I learn into something useful for people.

Instead of keeping my learning only in books and classrooms, I build mobile applications, websites, knowledge platforms, creative projects and community initiatives that can take ideas into the real world.

My philosophy

        LEARN
          ↓
      UNDERSTAND
          ↓
        BUILD
          ↓
        TEST
          ↓
        SHARE
          ↓
        IMPACT

The problem comes first. The technology follows.

📚 LEARNING IN PUBLIC

I am an Ayurveda student.

A lot of what I build begins with something I am studying.

Sometimes it starts with a classical Sanskrit text.

Sometimes with a medicinal plant.

Sometimes with a conversation with a community.

Sometimes with something I encounter at a climate conference.

Then I ask:

Can I build something useful from this?

That question has led to apps, websites, platforms, media projects and new ideas.
